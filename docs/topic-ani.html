ani

A phone number pool is a range of numbers, from its first to its last. A single number is a pool of its own. A pool is mapped to the trunks its numbers are dialled in on and presented from, and pools never overlap.

Each of a dialer's trunk rows presents caller IDs from a pool mapped to that trunk, and picks a number from it as the dialer says. A pool a dialer names cannot be deleted.

Numbers

  • Enter the first and last numbers in digits only, without +, both the same length.
  • For a single number, enter it as both the first and the last.
  • A range that overlaps another pool is refused.

Trunks

Map the pool to each trunk its numbers are used on, in and out alike, here or on the trunk's page. A dialer's trunk row offers only the pools mapped to its trunk, and a mapping a dialer relies on cannot be removed.

Older forms

A pool marked as the older form has a direction and a scope. It is shown as it is until the upgrade converts it.

Outbound caller IDs are the older caller IDs. The upgrade turns each into a one-number phone number pool. Until then they can be read and deleted, but not changed. To present another number, make a phone number pool for it and map it to the trunks it goes out on.
