team

A team groups agents who work together. The team decides where its agents appear in reports and in Live View. An agent belongs to one team, so adding an agent to a team removes them from their old one. An agent removed from a team has no team.

Team lead

  • Team lead username is the username of the person who supervises the team. It is optional.
  • Enable barge lets the team lead listen to a team member's call and join it if needed.

Outbound dialing

  • Enable dialing allows the team's agents to place outbound calls, and shows the settings below.
  • Trunk is the route outbound calls use.
  • CLI is the number shown to the person being called. The trunk's carrier must accept it.
  • Record calls records the team's outbound calls.

Team list

  • The number on each team is the number of agents in it.
  • The Barge, Dial and Record chips are highlighted when the team can listen to calls, place outbound calls, or record them.
