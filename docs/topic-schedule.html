schedule

A schedule held the hours in which the dialers on it could dial. Dialers now have their own hours, set on each dialer, and schedules are read only.

A dialer whose hours were never set still dials within the schedules it was on. Once its hours are set, its schedules no longer apply to it. A schedule cannot be deleted while a dialer still uses it.

Calling hours

The hours in which a dialer on the schedule can dial, for each day of the week, shown in your browser's time zone. The schedule is stored in UTC.

Other lines

Lines the week cannot show, such as a day of the month, are listed as cron expressions in UTC with five fields: minute, hour, day of month, month and weekday (Sunday is 0).

Commands

  • xema schedule add
  • xema schedule fields
  • xema schedule remove
  • xema schedule set
  • xema schedule show
  • xema schedules
