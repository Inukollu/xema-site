dialer

A dialer places outbound calls automatically. It works through a dataset of numbers at a set pace and connects answered calls to a queue's agents.

A running dialer cannot be edited. Stop it first.

Status

  • Live shows which dialers are dialing now.
  • Outside hours means the dialer is started but its hours do not allow dialing at this time. A dialer with no hours never dials.
  • Not dialing means the dialer is started and within its hours but is waiting, and Live says why, such as no idle agents or nothing left to dial.
  • Idle dialers are stopped.
  • In a dialer's own status, green means it is dialing. Amber means it is started but not dialing: either it is outside its hours, or it is waiting for something, such as an idle agent, and Xema says what. Grey means idle.
  • Test call places one call to a number you enter, without using the dataset.
  • The status refreshes every 15 seconds.

Dialing

  • Pace depends on the type. For an agentless dialer it is the calls placed each minute. For a power dialer it is the calls kept ringing for each free agent, a whole number from 1 to 20. A predictive dialer starts at one call for each free agent and predicts from there. Preview and progressive dialers place one call for each free agent.
  • An occupancy dialer has nothing to set. It forecasts like a predictive dialer but has no abandonment cap, so more answered callers can reach nobody. Check that the law where you are calling allows that.
  • Trunks are tried in the order listed. Each row presents caller IDs, the numbers shown to the people being called, from a phone number pool mapped to its trunk. A trunk can also limit how many of the dialer's calls ring on it at once.
  • A row offers only the pools mapped to its trunk; a pool is mapped on its own page. Choose how a number is picked from the pools for each call.
  • The dataset holds the numbers to call. The test dataset is a separate list for trial runs.
  • A manual dialer places the calls agents make themselves: their own calls, call backs and conference parties. It has no pace, dataset or hours, and is never started. Name it on a queue, a team, a process or the cluster settings.

Dialing hours

  • A dialer's hours are its own: for each day of the week, the windows in which it may dial. A day can have several windows, for example a morning and an afternoon. A window ending at 00:00 runs to the end of the day. A day with no window is closed.
  • The hours are read in the dialer's time zone. Leave the zone blank to use the process's time zone, or else the cluster's.
  • A dialer whose hours were never set still dials within the schedules it was on, until its hours are set. Press Set hours to give it its own.
  • Copy hours from puts another dialer's week and time zone in place of these, so dialers that keep the same hours are set alike. It offers only dialers you can see that have hours of their own. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

Datasets

  • Each batch shows how many records remain. Test batches belong to the test dataset, Target batches to the dataset.
  • Refresh datasets reloads them from the source. Allow a minute before refreshing.
