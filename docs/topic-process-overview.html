process-overview

One process at a glance: who works in it, the queues and dialers it uses, and the datasets it dials.

Reading the cards

  • The large figure is how many there are. Open a card to see that list.
  • A pencil means you may create items there. A lock means only a manager adds to it, though you may still remove from it.
  • An amber dot marks something that needs your attention.

Now

Each of the process's queues shows its callers waiting, the longest wait, its agents taking calls, how many calls were answered today, and how many callers gave up in the last ten minutes. Each dialer shows whether it is running, its pace, and how many calls it has completed today. The figures change as calls come and go.

Agent pauses, Agent UI panels and Dialers

Shown to a manager only. Agent pauses says how time on a ban or a break counts for the process's agents. Agent UI panels says which panels, such as Chat or My day, its agents see in Agent UI. Dialers says whether its dialers may use a test dataset. Each setting is As the cluster, On or Off, and As the cluster shows the cluster's value next to it. A process owner does not see these.

What a process owner may change

  • You may remove agents, teams, queues, dialers and datasets from your process. Nothing is deleted; they only leave the process.
  • You may create teams and datasets in it. You may create agents, queues and dialers where the process allows it.
  • Only a manager or an administrator adds an existing queue or dialer to a process.
