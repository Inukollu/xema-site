process

A process is a line of business: its agents, teams, queues, dialers and datasets, with owners who manage it and QAs who listen to its calls. Each process on a cluster has a name that is unique among processes, and optionally a description.

Settings

  • Allow listening: whether calls of the process's agents can be listened to. Listening also requires the team's own setting to be enabled.
  • Owners may add agents: owners can create new agents in the process.
  • Owners may add team leads: owners can set the lead of a team in the process.
  • Owners may add queues and Owners may add dialers: owners can create queues and dialers in the process.

These settings cover only creating new ones. An existing agent, queue or dialer is put in a process by a manager or an admin, never by an owner, whatever the settings say.

In this process

Available after the process is created. It lists the process's agents, queues and dialers. Add existing picks ones that are not in the process yet and puts them in. Remove takes one out of this process only, and deletes nothing.

A new process starts with the four owner settings enabled and listening disabled. In the list of processes, Owners may add shows what the owners can add themselves, and Listening shows whether calls can be listened to.

Scheduled calls

Available after the process is created. These are calls an agent books for later, such as at the end of a call.

  • Place scheduled calls: whether booked calls are placed.
  • Agent: who takes a call when it is due. Choose only the agent who booked it, that agent if they are free and otherwise any free agent, or any free agent.
  • Dialer: the dialer that places the calls. It must be one of the process's own dialers, and not an agentless one. Its kind sets the pacing. While it is named here, it cannot be taken out of the process or made agentless.

While scheduled calls are on, both the agent and the dialer are required. When they are off, the choices are kept for next time.

Owners and QAs

Available after the process is created. A user is added by username. Owners manage the process; QAs listen to its calls.

Agent alert

The prompt an agent hears as a call from one of the process's queues connects, dialed calls included. As a preference it plays where a queue names no alert of its own. As a policy it plays for every queue in the process. With none, the cluster's alert plays, or else a beep. Only managers change it.

Processes you look after

A manager sees every process. A process owner sees only the processes they own. Open a process to work in it.

Deleting a process

Its agents, teams, queues, dialers and datasets are not deleted, but are removed from the process. Its owners and QAs lose access to it. A process with workload plans cannot be deleted; delete the plans first.
