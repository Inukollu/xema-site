audio

An audio is a recording played to callers: a prompt, a menu, a thank-you message or a promotion. An audio can hold one recording per language, and the caller hears the one in their own language. Add a recording for every language your flows use.

Recordings

  • Languages shows which recordings each audio has.
  • Common audio formats are accepted (WAV, MP3, GSM). Files are converted for the phone system when they are uploaded.

Commands

  • xema audio add
  • xema audio fields
  • xema audio record
  • xema audio remove
  • xema audio set
  • xema audio show
  • xema audios
