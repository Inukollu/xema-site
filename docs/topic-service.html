service

Services are the systemd units on a server: Xema's own services and the infrastructure they depend on. The status is the same as xema status on the server, and starting, stopping and restarting a service runs xema start, stop and restart. The list refreshes every 15 seconds.

Status

  • The dot shows the unit's state: green running, red failed, amber changing, grey stopped.
  • Below the name are the systemd state, uptime, memory use and restart count. A rising restart count needs investigation even while the dot is green.

Before you stop a service

  • Calls in progress may drop. Stopping or restarting Asterisk, AsterMQ or FastAGI affects live calls immediately.
  • The infrastructure services support the whole platform. Stopping MariaDB or RabbitMQ stops Xema as well.
  • The Manager service serves Xema Manager, so it can be restarted from Xema Manager but not stopped. To stop it, use the command line on the server.
