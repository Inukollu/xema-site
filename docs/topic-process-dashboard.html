process-dashboard

Every process you look after, in one place. A manager sees every process. A process owner sees the processes they own. What it shows is what you may see.

Reading it

  • The figures at the top count across all your processes. An agent or a queue in two processes counts once.
  • Each process then shows its agents, teams, queues, dialers and datasets. Open one to work in it.
  • An amber dot marks something that needs doing, such as a team with no lead.
