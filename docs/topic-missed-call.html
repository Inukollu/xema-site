missed-call

A missed call is a call that arrived on a trunk for a number the trunk does not route: the number has no DNI on that trunk, or its DNI is disabled. These calls went no further than the trunk.

They are not calls abandoned in a queue or not answered by an agent. Those calls reached a routed number and entered its flow, so they appear in the call records with their outcome.

Fields

  • DNIS number is the number the caller dialled; CLI number is the caller's number.
  • Use the CLI to call the caller back.
  • A number that appears repeatedly as a missed call usually needs a DNI.
