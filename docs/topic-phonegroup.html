phonegroup

A phone group holds phones that share the same settings. Every phone belongs to a group, and the group sets what its phones can do. Barging, outbound dialling (with its trunk and caller ID) and recording are set on the group, not on the individual phone. A change to a group applies to every phone in it.

Settings

  • Enable barge lets supervisors listen in on or join calls on these phones.
  • Enable dial allows outbound calls and shows the related settings: the Trunk used for the calls, the CLI presented to the called party, and whether calls are recorded.

In the list

  • Phones shows how many phones are in each group.
  • When dialling is enabled, Outbound shows "Dial · Recording" with the trunk and caller ID below.

Commands

  • xema phonegroup add
  • xema phonegroup fields
  • xema phonegroup remove
  • xema phonegroup set
  • xema phonegroup show
  • xema phonegroups
