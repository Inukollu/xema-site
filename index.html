What it is

Everything a contact centre runs on, in one place.

Inbound and outbound, the agent's screen, the supervisor's view and the numbers you report on afterwards — one product rather than four that have to be made to agree.

Queues that choose

Callers are matched to agents on skills, not just on who is free. Wrap-up, pauses and ring-no-answer are handled by the queue rather than by whoever remembers to.

Call flows

Menus, prompts, business hours and callbacks, drawn as a flow. Look a caller up in your own data, or ask your systems a question, before anybody answers.

Outbound dialing

Dialers dial from your own datasets at a pace you set, and hand answered calls to the same queues and the same agents as everything else.

Live and historic

Wallboards for the cluster, and call records with the recording attached. Answered, abandoned, service level and every agent's day — kept as data you can export, not a screenshot of a dashboard.

Your servers

A cloud instance, a machine in your own rack, or a rack of them. Your recordings and your customers' details stay where you put them, under whichever rules you answer to.

Integrations

Screen-pop your CRM the moment a call connects, and drive the rest from an API — so the agent works in one window instead of three.

For agents

A phone in the browser, and nothing to install.

Agents sign in to a web page and take calls through it. Nothing to install on their machine, no handset to configure, and no particular desk they have to be sitting at — a browser and a headset is the whole of it.

  • Hold, transfer, conference and hang up, beside the customer's record.
  • Breaks that a supervisor can see, with wrap-up time the queue enforces.
  • Dispositions, call history and missed calls, per agent.
  • Barge, whisper and coaching for whoever is running the cluster.

Scale

Ten seats or a thousand, without starting again.

The same product runs a single team and a cluster spread across buildings. Growing means adding capacity, not migrating to a different edition and rebuilding what you already configured.

One cluster, several locations

Agents in different offices share the same queues, the same reporting and the same supervisors. Where somebody is sitting stops being an operational question.

Built to keep answering

Capacity is spread across machines, so losing one does not take the phones down with it and maintenance does not have to wait for a quiet weekend.

No per-seat licence

Add agents to a dialer and take them off afterwards. What it costs to run is what your servers cost, not what your headcount does.
